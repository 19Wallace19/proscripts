TikTok Optimizer Pro — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 16, 2026

1. Information We Collect

TikTok Optimizer Pro does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. All data is stored locally on your device.

2. Device Permissions

Photo Library Access: To allow you to select videos from your camera roll for optimization analysis. We do not upload, share, or transmit your videos or photos to any server.

Clipboard Access: To copy generated hooks, hashtags, and descriptions for your convenience when posting to TikTok or other platforms.

3. Data Storage

All app data (generated hooks, hashtags, history, favorites, and notes) is stored locally on your device using secure local storage. No data is transmitted to our servers or third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

The app includes an optional deep link to CapCut video editor. Opening CapCut is subject to CapCut's own privacy policy and terms of service. We do not share any data with CapCut.

5. Data Deletion

You can delete all locally stored data at any time by using the "Clear All History" feature in the app, or by uninstalling the app from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
