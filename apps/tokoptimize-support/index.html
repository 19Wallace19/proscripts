Common Questions

Q: The app crashes when I select a video.
A: Make sure you've granted photo library access in Settings → Privacy → Photos.

Q: CapCut won't open.
A: Install CapCut from the App Store first. The deep link only works if CapCut is installed.

Q: How do I delete my history?
A: Go to the History tab → tap "Clear All History" at the bottom.

Q: Is my data shared with TikTok?
A: No. All data stays on your device. We don't connect to TikTok's servers.
