ProTok Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

1. Information We Collect

ProTok Studio does not collect any personal data.

All app functionality runs locally on your device. We do not have servers that store your information.

2. Local Data Storage

The app stores the following data only on your device:

  • Your scheduled content reminders
  • Your brand/niche profile settings
  • Cached trend data (refreshed weekly)

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it.

3. Third-Party Services

ProTok Studio uses the following third-party services:

  • GitHub: To fetch weekly trending hashtag and sound data
  • Expo: For app build and distribution services

These services may collect anonymized usage data according to their own privacy policies.

4. Camera and Photos Access

The app requests access to your camera and photo library only to allow you to import videos for editing. We do not access, upload, or store your photos or videos on any server.

5. Push Notifications

If you enable notifications, the app uses local device notifications to remind you of scheduled posts. These notifications are handled entirely by your device's operating system.
